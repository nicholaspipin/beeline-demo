BeeLine — Privacy Policy

Effective: September 1, 2026 · Test version

The short version: BeeLine has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no server of its own. Your data stays on your phone. BeeLine never sells or shares your personal information — there's nothing collected to sell.

1. What stays on your device (everything personal)

All of your personal data lives in the app's local storage on your device and is never uploaded anywhere by BeeLine:

  • Your class schedule (courses, rooms, times) that you type in
  • Your saved places and quick destinations
  • Your walk history and "learned lines" (the routes and times the app measures as you walk)
  • Your settings and preferences

Deleting it: delete the app and it's all gone. You can also remove individual classes, places, and walks inside the app.

2. Location

BeeLine asks for location permission to show where you are, point you toward destinations, and time your walks. Location is processed live on your device. Walk paths you record are stored on your device only (see §1). BeeLine has no server that receives or stores your location history.

3. What leaves your device — and why

Like any map app, BeeLine contacts a few third-party services only to answer things you ask for. Each of these receives your device's IP address (that's how the internet works) plus only what's listed:

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
CARTO (basemaps.cartocdn.com)Map-tile requests for the area you're viewingDrawing the map
OSRM / Valhalla (routing.openstreetmap.de, valhalla1.openstreetmap.de)Start and end coordinates of a walk you ask aboutWalking time and route estimates
Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org)Coordinates of a place you openAddress and details lookup
Photon (photon.komoot.io)Text you type in search, campus coordinatesFinding places beyond the built-in campus list
IU Campus Bus feed (iucbs.etaspot.net)Standard requests only — no personal dataLive bus positions and schedules
Wikimedia (upload.wikimedia.org)Standard image requestsBuilding photos
Expo Updates (expo.dev)Standard app-update check (app id, version)Delivering app updates
Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com)Approximate coordinates (campus area)The weather chip on Home

None of these requests include your name, your schedule, your saved places, or your walk history. BeeLine adds no identifiers of its own. These services have their own privacy policies; BeeLine doesn't control them.

4. What BeeLine's developer can see

Essentially nothing about you. There is no analytics dashboard, no usage tracking, no crash reporter, and no database of users. If you email for help, your email is seen — that's it.

5. Children

BeeLine is intended for college students and adults (17+). It is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes

If BeeLine ever changes how data is handled (for example, adding a feature that stores anything off-device), this policy will be updated first and the change will be called out in the app's update notes.
